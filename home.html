Map the property, plan stands and burns, and log every hunt. Free, with no accounts.

On your PC

The hub, where the mapping and planning happens. Windows 10 or 11.

Download for Windows

About 120 MB.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

That's normal for a free app that isn't code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. It installs just for you, with no admin password.

On your phone

Drop GPS pins, log hunts from the stand, and open stands in Apple Maps. Works with no signal.

Open the field page
  1. Open this page on your iPhone in Safari and tap Open the field page.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it once while you have signal, and allow location when it asks.

Sharing with family

  • Property maps: open a property in the hub and press Share, or use Export whole map. Send the file; they use Import a map file.
  • Hunts and GPS pins: in the hub, open Settings › Camp sync and start a camp. Everyone types the camp code on the phone page's Camp tab, and their hunts and pins reach the hub by themselves. Stands go to their phones the same way.

Maps stay on your own PC. Camp hunts and pins are kept in a free Firebase database that only people with your camp code can open.
