# Ideas for later

Not started. Everything must stay free (public data or what's already recorded).

## Top three

1. **Wind and scent map:** pick a stand and the forecast wind, and the map draws where your scent blows, flagging bedding areas, deer trails and the walk in that it crosses. Builds on stand winds and Hunt Forecast.
2. **Harvest and herd records:** log each deer's sex, age, weight, antlers and location. Over seasons: buck-to-doe ratio, age structure, doe harvest goals. Trail camera counts could add a yearly herd estimate.
3. **Habitat work calendar:** a yearly plan by section for burns, timber work, food plots and invasive control, with each section's history and reminders when work is due.

## Hunter

- **Stand pressure:** warn when a stand has been hunted too often lately, from hunt reports.
- **Pattern finder:** which wind, temperature, moon and time of day produced the most sightings, by stand and by buck.
- **Buck profiles:** a timeline and map for each named buck, from camera photos and sightings.
- **Quiet access routes:** ways to a stand that stay downwind and out of sight of bedding areas, using lidar terrain.

## Land manager

- **Soil maps:** USDA soil data for each food plot, suggested plantings, and a log for lime, fertilizer and soil tests.
- **Food plot records:** what was planted and when, how it grew, how much deer use it.
- **Burn day planner:** for a burn unit, wind, humidity and smoke direction from forecasts, plus firebreaks and a checklist. Helps planning only; the burn plan and local rules decide when it's safe.
- **Timber stands:** species, age and condition for each stand, with thinning dates and notes.
- **Spending and work log:** hours, costs and equipment by project, also useful for cost-share paperwork.

## Conservationist

- **Wildlife log beyond deer:** turkey, quail, bear, songbirds, pollinators and rare plants, from pins and camera photos. MegaDetector only finds animals, people and vehicles, so species are named by hand.
- **Before-and-after photo points:** fixed spots rephotographed each year to show how a burn or thinning changed the woods.
- **Invasive species tracker:** map patches of plants like sericea lespedeza, log treatments, see whether each patch shrinks.
- **Water features:** springs, ponds and streams, with water levels and notes on streamside buffers.
- **CWD records:** which deer were sampled and where (Missouri's Ozarks have CWD management zones).
